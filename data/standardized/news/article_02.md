# Cam nang du lich Phu Quoc

---

Phu Quoc is an island destination with beaches, night markets, seafood restaurants, and resort areas. A practical guide should mention the dry season, airport access, island transport, snorkeling areas, family-friendly beaches, and local specialties. Travelers should plan enough time for distance between the north, central, and southern parts of the island.
