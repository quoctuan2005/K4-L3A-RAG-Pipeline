# Cam nang du lich Hoi An

**Source:** https://vnexpress.net/cam-nang-du-lich-hoi-an-4446174.html

**Crawled:** 2026-09-20T00:00:00

---

Hoi An is known for its ancient town, lantern streets, riverside walks, tailor shops, local dishes, and cycling routes to nearby villages. A travel guide should highlight ticketed heritage areas, early morning markets, evening crowds, nearby beaches, and respectful behavior in old houses and assembly halls. Many visitors combine Hoi An with Da Nang.
