# University Tuition and Fee Policy

**Source:** tuition_policy.doc

Tuition is billed by semester and payment is due before the published deadline unless the student has an approved payment plan. Students remain responsible for unpaid balances after scholarships or financial aid are applied. Refunds depend on the official withdrawal date, and some administrative or service fees may be non-refundable. Appeals must include the student ID, term, charge description, supporting documents, and a clear explanation of the requested adjustment.
