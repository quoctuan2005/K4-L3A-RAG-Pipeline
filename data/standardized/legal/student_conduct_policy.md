# Student Conduct and Academic Integrity Policy

**Source:** student_conduct_policy.doc

Academic integrity requires students to submit their own work, cite sources accurately, and follow assessment instructions. Prohibited behavior includes plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, impersonation, and use of unauthorized materials during examinations. Conduct reports may lead to review, evidence gathering, student response, and sanctions such as warning, probation, restitution, suspension, or expulsion. Appeals must identify a recognized basis such as procedural error or new information.
